The "Black Box" Problem

Main Idea 3

Nestor and Aaron explain the Black Box problem. Many AI models give answers or make decisions, but even the programmers cannot clearly see how the system got that result.

The Loss of Human Control

Main Idea 4

If AI systems become too complex and take decisions by themselves, humans could lose control over important processes in society and technology.

Final Conclusion

We think this is a very important topic to talk about these days, because it will have a big impact in the future of AI and technology.
