The "Dead Internet Theory"

Main Idea 1

In the podcast, Sebas, Nestor and Aaron talk about the Dead Internet Theory. This idea says that a lot of the content and accounts we see on the internet today are made by bots and AI instead of real people.

Mass Surveillance and the End of Public Anonymity

Main Idea 2

We also discuss how artificial intelligence is used for mass surveillance. With facial recognition and data tracking, public anonymity is disappearing and privacy is harder to keep.
